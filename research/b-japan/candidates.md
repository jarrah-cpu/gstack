## SURPLUS snowball findings

- **Rainbow Disco Club:** a direct and active link. It programmed SURPLUS 2026 in Sydney, runs its home festival in Izu each April, and is expanding with a Singapore debut in Oct 2026 and an edition at Potato Head Bali.
- **Breakfast Club Tokyo:** a 1950s-style American diner in Higashiyama, Meguro (owner Luli Shioi), not an event. It has done pop-ups abroad, including one with a DJ at Basehall 02, Jardine House, Hong Kong. Sources: [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/restaurants/breakfast-club), [Time Out Hong Kong](https://www.timeout.com/hong-kong/things-to-do/breakfast-club-tokyo-at-basehall-02).
- **Menchirashi:** a Harajuku udon restaurant, not an event. It has fashion tie-ups, including the sacai THE noodle pop-up at Landmark BaseHall, Hong Kong (20 to 28 Oct 2023). Sources: [Hypebae](https://hypebae.com/2023/10/sacai-the-noodle-menchirashi-dining-pop-up-landmark-hong-kong-hours-opening-info), [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/restaurants/menchirashi).
- **Nike Sportswear and New Balance:** both exhibit at atmoscon 2026 (Shinjuku, 31 Oct to 1 Nov) alongside Jordan Brand, adidas Originals and ASICS SportStyle. atmoscon is the Japanese event that most resembles SURPLUS in structure: a sneaker retailer curating brands, music and a runway. [Have a Good Holiday](https://www.haveagood-holiday.com/en/articles/atmoscon-2026-urban-culture-festival-shinjuku-sumitomo-building)
- **Deus Ex Machina:** its Japan blog lists a presence at GREENROOM FESTIVAL 2025 and brand-run events (MINI JCW x Deus, a Deus x Attaquer six-city ride, Bayside at the Emporium Vol.2). [Deus Japan events](https://jp.deuscustoms.com/blogs/news/tagged/event)
- **Carhartt WIP:** in Japan the visible activity is content, not events: the Inshin Denshin skate video shot across Tokyo, Osaka and Nagoya. [Quartersnacks](https://quartersnacks.com/2026/01/after-party-in-family-mart-inshin-denshin-carhartt-wip-in-japan/)
- **Vans:** no 2026 Japan culture event found. Its last visible Japan festival was the Duct Tape surf festival at Kugenuma in 2019 (see Leads).
- **Pattern:** SURPLUS's Tokyo food vendors travel through fashion-led pop-ups rather than Japanese festivals. Big sportswear brands show up in Japan through retailer festivals (atmoscon) and contests (X Games), while lifestyle brands like Deus attach to GREENROOM-style surf culture.

## Gaps

The top-up pass used its full 36 searches, so coverage is now broad but shallow. Longboard and downhill skating returned nothing in Japan, fixed-gear culture looks shop-led with no festival format found, and HINOYA CAMP did not surface in two searches. Van life only appears as camper vehicle fairs and climbing only as competitions. No Miyazaki surf culture festival was found, only contests, and Chiba and Shonan beyond the existing records produced only past or one-off events. Zushi Beach Film Festival, the strongest new find, has no sponsor roster or pinned attendance yet. Only one Instagram handle (Furugi Fes) was captured and no follower counts. Attendance is missing for most new records, and FIELDSTYLE's 50,000 is a media-quoted figure. Next editions for 2027 are mostly inferred from past patterns; the Yokohama Hot Rod Custom Show date still rests on a third-party listing, and NEW ORDER CHOPPER SHOW rests on a single listing. Some sponsor and price attributions (atmoscon exhibitors, JAPAN SAUNA FESTIVAL sponsors, Yoga Festa programme) come from search summaries and should be checked against the cited pages in Phase 2. A follow-up should target Zushi's partners, atmoscon and DESIGNART partner lists, the sauna roundup, and the Sanei calendar.
