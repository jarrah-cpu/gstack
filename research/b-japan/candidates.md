## SURPLUS snowball findings

- **Rainbow Disco Club:** a direct and active link. It programmed SURPLUS 2026 in Sydney, runs its home festival in Izu each April, and is expanding with a Singapore debut in Oct 2026 and an edition at Potato Head Bali.
- **Breakfast Club Tokyo:** a 1950s-style American diner in Higashiyama, Meguro (owner Luli Shioi), not an event. It has done pop-ups abroad, including one with a DJ at Basehall 02, Jardine House, Hong Kong. Sources: [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/restaurants/breakfast-club), [Time Out Hong Kong](https://www.timeout.com/hong-kong/things-to-do/breakfast-club-tokyo-at-basehall-02).
- **Menchirashi:** a Harajuku udon restaurant, not an event. It has fashion tie-ups, including the sacai THE noodle pop-up at Landmark BaseHall, Hong Kong (20 to 28 Oct 2023). Sources: [Hypebae](https://hypebae.com/2023/10/sacai-the-noodle-menchirashi-dining-pop-up-landmark-hong-kong-hours-opening-info), [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/restaurants/menchirashi).
- **Pattern:** SURPLUS's Tokyo food vendors travel through fashion-led pop-ups (sacai, Basehall Hong Kong) rather than Japanese festivals. They are better seen as collaborators than as event leads.

## Gaps

The search budget ran out after 17 queries, so many starting leads were never checked. No search was run for Mooneyes Street Car Nationals, atmos or Sneaker Con Tokyo, Tokyo Design Week, DESIGNART Tokyo, Snow Peak or Hinoya outdoor events, or fixed-gear and gravel cycling. Skateboarding and downhill, tattoo conventions, climbing festivals, wellness, street art, van life, Tokyo zine fests and Miyazaki surf festivals were not searched either. No Instagram handles or follower counts were captured. No sponsor rosters were found beyond GO OUT CAMP's media, venue and council partners and RDC's ticketing partner. No partnerships contacts were found except Sanei's sampling plan PDF. Several next-edition dates are inferred from past patterns. The Yokohama Hot Rod Custom Show date (6 Dec 2026) rests on a third-party listing. A follow-up run should cover the unchecked leads above and look for sponsor and IG data on the top picks.
