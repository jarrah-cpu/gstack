## Gaps

This region is badly under-searched. The shared search budget ran out after 15 queries, so the set skews to electronic and indie music and to independent publishing in São Paulo and Buenos Aires. Peru has no candidates at all, and Chile only has two imported or mass-market music events. None of the surf, longboard and downhill skate, skateboarding, sneaker and streetwear, van life, climbing, gravel and fixed-gear, tattoo, custom moto, wellness or street art strands were searched, and these are where the strongest High Ground fits are likely to be. No Instagram handles, follower counts, attendance figures, partnership contacts or ticket prices were captured for any record. Several statuses (Feira Plana, Buenos Aires Fanzine Fest, DGTL Santiago, Dekmantel São Paulo, Time Warp Buenos Aires) rest on absence of recent coverage and need confirming. A follow-up pass with a fresh search budget should start with the unchecked starting leads above, in Spanish and Portuguese.
