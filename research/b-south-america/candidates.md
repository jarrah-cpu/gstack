## Gaps

Coverage is now broad but shallow. Every strand in the region brief was searched at least once, and Peru (9) and Chile (13) now have real records, but many records rest on one or two sources and most lack Instagram handles, attendance, sponsors and prices (only Lobos por Siempre has a handle). The SURPLUS snowball found nothing: no Carhartt WIP, Nike Sportswear, Rainbow Disco Club or Mixmag activity surfaced in São Paulo, Buenos Aires or Santiago. Strands that returned no qualifying event: climbing (no Cochamó or Patagonia gathering), downhill skate (the 2026 world championships were in Paraguay; nothing in the four countries beyond a tiny Pará contest), log and single-fin festivals in Brazil, Chicama and Máncora events, Buenos Aires and Santiago independent streetwear fairs, custom motorcycle shows of the Deus kind, and wellness festivals in Peru. Van life and overland is a single club rally. Several dates need checking: Sneakerness Santiago (organiser site only), MOSPERÚ (page gives three different date ranges), Virada Zen (listing has no year) and Gravel del Fuego. Statuses resting on absence of coverage remain for Lobos por Siempre, STU Open Rio, Floripa Surf Film Festival, Lima Tattoo Convention, Sneaker Con São Paulo and Huanchaco, plus the first-pass set (Feira Plana, Buenos Aires Fanzine Fest, DGTL Santiago, Dekmantel São Paulo, Time Warp Buenos Aires). Phase 2 should start with GCA, Surfestival, FED, Selvámonos and Go Skateboarding Day Perú, which have the strongest culture fit but no captured partner or contact data.
