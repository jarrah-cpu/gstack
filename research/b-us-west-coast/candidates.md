## Gaps

The top-up pass widened the region from music and starting leads to all twelve strands in the brief, but most new records rest on one or two search results. No Instagram handle appeared in any result, so every IG field is still null for Phase 2. Partnership contacts are null throughout. Named sponsors are still thin: the new records add only Spotify (FORM) and seven sauna-maker vendors (Seattle Sauna Festival). Attendance exists for only a handful: MADE's 7,000+ is organiser-reported, FORM's 2,500 is a capacity cap, Red Rock Rendezvous and Bhakti Fest figures are undated, and Burning Man's 70,000+ is a third-party estimate. Several organisers are unnamed (Sessions Surf Festival, LOGJAM!, KASSO, Wild Soul, the Tahoe wellness events), and Sessions in particular needs an organiser and partner check because it is the closest SURPLUS-style format found. Some dates conflict or come only from aggregators: Descend on Bend (1 to 4 Sep vs the Labor Day weekend), Belgian Waffle Ride 2027, Musink 2027, Climb Smart (directory source) and Burning Man 2027 (planning site quoting the organiser). Status is unclear for eight records (Agenda, Smith Rock Craggin' Classic, Red Rock Rendezvous, Bhakti Fest, Musink, L.A. Zine Fest, Vans Duct Tape, Maryhill Festival of Speed). Brand or retailer-run streetwear block parties, the nearest US analogue to SURPLUS, did not surface at all and may need Instagram-led discovery in Phase 2.
