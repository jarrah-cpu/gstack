## Gaps

All thirteen brief subcultures now have at least one record, but depth is uneven. Downhill and longboard skate is still the weakest strand: no Australian downhill race is confirmed for 2026 or 2027, and the only leads are dated. Fixed-gear and alleycat culture produced no current events at all (alleycats are announced on short notice through crews and shops, which search does not reach). No community-format overlanding festival was found, so the only overland record is a trade show. No outdoor climbing gathering was found for the Grampians, Arapiles or Blue Mountains; climbing bans at Arapiles since November 2024 may explain part of that. Wonderwalls appears dormant after 2017 and the Australian Wanderlust events after about 2023, but neither has a source confirming an end. Across the 35 new records no Instagram handles or follower counts appeared in results, no partnership contacts were found, and crowd figures exist only for Strawberry Fields and Sea Otter. Several dates rest on one aggregator or a search summary (Rainbow Spirit 2027, GreazeFest 2027, The Big Sweat price, Dungog Pedalfest, Gears and Beers) and need verification in Phase 2. The Distinguished Gentleman's Ride 2027 date is still unannounced after a targeted search. Many 2026 editions are marked held from their announced dates without post-event coverage.
