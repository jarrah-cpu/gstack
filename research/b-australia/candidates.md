## Gaps

Coverage is the main gap. Only four of the brief's thirteen subcultures were searched (surf, skate, custom moto, tattoo). Electronic music and bush doofs, van life, climbing, gravel and fixed-gear cycling, wellness and breathwork, street art and zines have no records, and the target of 15 to 40 candidates was not reached. Within the ten records: no Instagram handles or follower counts were seen in search results. No partnership contacts, ticket prices or sponsor rosters were found, except Vans naming Bowl-a-rama. No crowd figures were found either, only competitor or exhibitor counts. The Noosa 2027 dates and the Tattoo Expo's Perth and Melbourne dates each rest on a single third-party listing. The Distinguished Gentleman's Ride 2027 date is unresolved: one source says it may move to a Northern Hemisphere autumn date. Skate Australia paused new event sanctioning in 2025, and no dedicated Australian downhill race was found for 2026 or 2027. A follow-up run with fresh search budget should start with the unsearched leads listed above.
