## Gaps

The search budget was exhausted after 12 queries, so this region is under-covered against the 15 to 40 target and skews to Adelaide. Not done: the SURPLUS brand snowball (Nike, Supply, Carhartt WIP, Mixmag, Heaps Normal, Young Henrys, Red Bull, Vans) in either city; Perth club culture and Fremantle; Margaret River; Fringe World and Perth Festival. No IG handles or follower counts appeared in any result, so every instagram block is null. Only one sponsor was confirmed (Aesop at Adelaide Design Week). No partnership contacts were found apart from a 2021 organiser email for Zina Warrior Print Fest. Several official websites (Adelaide Fringe, SALA, Illuminate Adelaide, Perth Design Week) were left null because their URLs did not appear in results. XChange Markets dates rest on a search summary attributed to a Glam Adelaide market guide that was not opened. A follow-up pass should start with the unsearched leads above.
