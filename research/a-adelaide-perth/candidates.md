## Gaps

Club culture in both cities is mostly venue-led and promoted on Instagram and Humanitix, so search found venues and one-off nights more often than named crews; Sidebyside, Unsound and Queer Club are the only crew-run events confirmed. The SURPLUS snowball came back almost empty: no Nike, Supply, Carhartt WIP, Vans, Converse, Heaps Normal, Young Henrys or Mixmag events were found in Adelaide or Perth. Red Bull appears only at Da Klinic's Go Skateboarding Day. Margaret River Pro side programs (music, art, surf culture) did not show up in any result, and no Perth surf film or log festival was found apart from the Lavan Whalebone Classic. Official websites for Illuminate Adelaide, Perth Design Week, Sidebyside and Froth and Fury still did not appear as result URLs. Fringe World 2027 dates come from a venue page and an IG mirror, not a press release. Only one IG handle (@fringeworldperth) appeared in results. Wellness events are thin: Othership's Runners' Night is the only run and sauna event found; the rest are venues or weekly run clubs. Partnership contacts, sponsor rosters and attendance remain largely null. Next pass should open Instagram and Humanitix pages for the club leads and confirm 2027 dates for Lucky Dumpling Market, Bowerbird (Nov 2026), Froth and Fury, Electric Island and Southern Rumble.
