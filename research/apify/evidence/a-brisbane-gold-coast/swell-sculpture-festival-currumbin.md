When is SWELL 2026?
We look forward to seeing you for #SWELL2026 between **11 – 20 September, 2026.**
The exhibition is open all hours so you can enjoy the sculptures at any time. There are Information Hubs along the 1.5km walkway where you can obtain information about the exhibition, what’s on, purchase raffle tickets, purchase a printed program and more. Information Hubs are open daily from 8am – 5pm.
