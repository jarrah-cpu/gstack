### https://www.blaksocial.com/ (BLAK SOCIAL)
Alethea Beetson (Kabi Kabi/Gubbi Gubbi and Wiradjuri) established Blak Social after successfully founding renowned Indigenous youth arts organisation, Digi Youth Arts, to continue creating spaces for the sovereign-led development and presentation of Indigenous stories and connected arts practice.
Since its beginnings in 2020, Blak Social has delivered productions such as Queen’s City, Blak Friday and held a residency at The Tivoli through which it delivered Blak Day Out 2022 and 2023 along with other music centric events. In 2023 Blak Social produced First Nations led alt-rock band Selve’s visual album Red Desert Dream via our label; and will be releasing new music through Blak Social reco
We acknowledge the important role the arts has played, and continues to play on these lands since the very first sunrise. We give thanks to the Elders, activists and artists who paved the way for us to do this work.
