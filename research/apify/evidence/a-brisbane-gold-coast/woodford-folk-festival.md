## Tickets and Car Passes
### [How much are tickets?](https://woodfordfolkfestival.com/info/faqs/#)
The ticket prices for this year’s Woodford Folk Festival can be found on the [Ticket Prices page](https://woodfordfolkfestival.com/ticket-prices/).
### [How can I buy tickets?](https://woodfordfolkfestival.com/info/faqs/#)
All tickets are to be purchased online using your Mastercard or Visa Card or Visa/Master Card Debit Card from the  [Tickets page](https://woodfordfolkfestival.com/tickets/).
If you prefer, you can call our office on 07 5496 1066 between 9am and 5pm AEST Monday to Friday and we will process your purchase over the phone. A credit or debit card will be required for all phone transactions, and will be processed on our secure online website.
### [What is a day ticket?](https://woodfordfolkfestival.com/info/faqs/#)
If you purchase a Day ticket, it will be valid just for one day of the festival. It allows you entry from 8.00am on the day of your ticket, until the end of the programme that night/early morning (approx. 2am). If you’d like to participate in any of the early morning activities, such as 6am yoga, you’ll need a Season ticket.
### [What is a season ticket?](https://woodfordfolkfestival.com/info/faqs/#)
A Season ticket is valid for all 6 days of the festival, 27 Dec – 1 Jan.

Overnight Camping is available on-site, with shower and toilet facilities, plus a Café for all your basic supplies. Your overnight camping ticket gives you access to the Festival Site from 8am on the day of your ticket, but please note that re-entry into the festival precinct the following morning is not permitted.
Overnight campers need to leave the camping areas by 10am. If you’d like to extend your stay, additional tickets are available at the Overnight Ticket Office located at the Happy Campers tent and open daily from 28th December to 1st January, between 7am and 2pm.
The festival is conducted in the full glory of summer. Our weather can range from very hot and dusty days through to impressive summer thunderstorms. FYI – it gets pretty chilly if you’re still wandering about enjoying The Pineapple Lounge at four in the morning so come prepared for whatever the weather may bring.
Powered Sites are available in the Season Campgrounds for Season with Camping ticket holders only. Powered Sites are an optional add-on to your season with camping ticket.
