# Contact Council
For enquiries, feedback or to report issues, there are various ways to contact Council.
## How to contact Council
Contact Council 24 hours a day, 7 days a week on [07 3403 8888](tel:0734038888 "Call Council").
Talk to Council in person at [Council’s Customer Centres](https://www.brisbane.qld.gov.au/about-council/contact-council#Customer), which provide information and advice on a range of topics, including:
*   rates enquiries
### ![](/content/dam/brisbanecitycouncil/common/icons/email.svg)By email
You can email Council using the [online formopens in a new tab](https://www.brisbane.qld.gov.au/online-services/council-services-and-requests/email-council "Email Council form").
If your request is complex or requires investigation, your email will be forwarded to the relevant business area to provide you with a written response within 30 days.
