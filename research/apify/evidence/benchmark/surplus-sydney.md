SURPLUS® – SURPLUS® 2026
Returning for it's second year, SURPLUS® is a multi-day, multi-event festival in Darlinghurst, Sydney, presented by SUPPLY and NIKE SPORTSWEAR, bringing together established and emerging creative communities, brands and people within SUPPLY STORE'S orbit. Connecting local to global, SURPLUS brings the online, offline.
MARKETPLACE across Burton St, Foley St and special Pop-Up tenancies hosts personalities, communities and brands within the SUPPLY STORE network. Each are releasing their own special lines and works.
Is it really free to attend OR do I need to get tickets? +
There are multiple events happening between the 16th and the 18th of October. Majority of SURPLUS events are open to the public and free to attend. Some events such as Shows are Ticketed or RSVP only. Follow events on "What's On" for more detailed information.
SCARR'S PIZZA NYC takes over AP PIZZA at Taylor Square for the 2nd year. MATTY'S PATTY'S BURGER CLUB returns to Marketplace, led by Canada's Top Chef Coulson Armstrong to serve their Double Smash Burger. MENCHIRASHI from Tokyo will be offering their viral Carbonara Udon. BoTT have Sydney local's YAKITORI YURIPPI at their Natsu Matsuri stalls on Burton St. All items available until sold out.
Please contact crimson@leafcomms.com for any press or media enquiries.
