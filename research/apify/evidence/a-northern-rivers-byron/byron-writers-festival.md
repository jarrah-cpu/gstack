What are the dates for the 2026 Byron Writers Festival?
Friday 14 – Sunday 16 August 2026.
In 2026, Byron Writers Festival will be held across multiple venues in the township of Byron Bay.
The following venues will be hosting 2026 Byron Writers Festival sessions and events: Byron Community Centre (Byron Theatre and Wategos Room), Beach Hotel, Byron Bay Surf Club, Johnson Street Stage, Haven, with more to be announced.
There will be a festival shuttle between the Cavanbah Centre and the precinct allowing our festival attendees to park and ride. The shuttle will drop off and pick up patrons from the Byron Bus Interchange.
Will I need separate tickets for each venue or is my festival pass valid everywhere?
Single-day and three-day passes are valid for programming between 9am and 5pm _only._ Feature events are separately ticketed and your day pass is not valid for these events.
