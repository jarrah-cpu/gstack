Mullum Roots Festival is held in four different venues in Mullumbimby town. The Mullumbimby High School Auditorium, The Drill Hall, The Courthouse Hotel, The Ex- Services Club and The Civic Hall. A ticket gets you a wristband to enter the venues. \*\* Please note that photo ID is required when entering the bowling club and the ex-services club.
The Love Bus is for shuttling in between venues throughout the weekend. There isn't a timetable for this as it will continuously run. The Love Bus will also pick up and drop back patrons from both camp grounds in Mullumbimby at least 3 times per day. #### What do I do with my ticket if I can't make it to the festival
You are welcome to gift your ticket to someone else. You don't need to change the name of the ticket holder, but they will need to present the ticket to be scanned at the Box Office. There are no refunds for tickets.
#### Do I have to buy a separate ticket if I want to go the Friday night opening concert?
Yes. Please present these upon arrival at the Box Office.
