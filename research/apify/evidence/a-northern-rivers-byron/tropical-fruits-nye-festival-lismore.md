**TICKETING FAQs**
**What are the festival ticket options?**
Tickets available for purchase online include:
*   Camping (Tickets based on the date of arrival)
Party tickets do not include camping. Camping tickets do not grant access to the parties.
Member discounts available on party tickets (current financial members of Tropical Fruits only). Discounts only apply to Member's own ticket.
Camping can be booked alongside your party tickets.
To secure your spot simply purchase a camping ticket for each person wishing to camp. Due to demand and capacity limitations all camping must be pre-booked online before arrival, with camping likely to book out before the festival begins.
**Can I book camping without booking a party ticket?**
No. Camping can only be booked by those who have purchased Party tickets (or registered as a volunteer). Any bookings found to not have a matching party ticket or volunteer registration may be cancelled and refunded at the discretion of the committee.

Yes, you can change the names on your ticket via Humanitix.
