In 1982 Jeff Prichard saw the Bangalow Showgrounds as the perfect location for an outdoor market. Large shady trees, parking space, power and toilet facilities and a community that welcomed the alternative nature and excitement the markets would bring. He organised and cajoled forty stallholders who made up the first market.

# Casual Stallholder Information
Please note that submitting this form is an application only. Casual stalls can be in high demand, and we often receive more applications than we have sites available. We appreciate your interest and thank you for your patience while we work through all applications.
All applicants will be notified by email by the end of Wednesday October 21st with the outcome of their application. Please wait for this email before following up, as we may not be able to confirm anything earlier. The number of sites available for casuals depends on how many permanent stallholders attend each month, and we won’t know final numbers until closer to the market date.
*   Only standard 3m x 3m stall spaces are available for casuals.
*   The fee for a casual site is $50, payable once your place is confirmed.
*   Power is not available for casual stalls, and generators are not permitted under any circumstances.
*   Sending an email asking for a casual site is not an application.
