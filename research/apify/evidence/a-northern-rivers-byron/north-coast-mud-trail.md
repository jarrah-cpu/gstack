NCC hosts three-four major annual events: the [NCC Surface Award exhibition (January)](https://www.northcoastceramics.com/award), an Easter Ceramic Market (March/ April), the celebrated [Mud Trail (August)](https://www.northcoastceramics.com/mud-trail), now in its thirteenth year, which opens studios to visitors, and the [Pre-Christmas Ceramic Market (November).](https://www.northcoastceramics.com
Alongside these events, NCC supports members and the public with professional development programs and exhibition opportunities year-round, fostering a thriving community passionate about clay and creativity.
North Coast Ceramics (NCC) is the peak body for ceramics in the Northern Rivers, representing professional and semi-professional ceramicists committed to excellence in their craft. Our rigorous membership criteria ensure that the public can trust they are purchasing from educated, experienced, and professional ceramic artists.
**For just $50 per year you can become an NCC member** and gain your own [dedicated space on this website](https://www.northcoastceramics.com/members-gallery). **Membership is valid from 1st January to 31st December** each year and is open to ceramicists with studios located in the **Byron, Ballina, or Lismore shires**.
