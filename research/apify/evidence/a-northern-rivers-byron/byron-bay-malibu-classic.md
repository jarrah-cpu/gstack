# **18th & 19th April 2026**
The Byron Bay Malibu Classic is an iconic event, and the Clubs main event of the year. Held at Wategos the contest attracts competitors from up and down the East Coast and regularly has a contingent visiting from New Zealand. A **single fin only 9ft** **and over** Malibu contest, it was first held in 1980, making it the longest-running event of its kind in Australia, perhaps the world.
Money raised at the Classic through the generosity of our sponsors and competitors has enabled the club to donate well over three hundred thousand dollars to local community organisations over the years.

The Byron Bay Malibu Club is a sporting club that meets on the second Sunday of every month for friendly competition, followed by a barbeque. At these days there is no set competing divisions and the emphasis is on fun on the waves for young and old. The club caters for all levels of surfing ability from beginners to State & Aussie Champions and we are proud to be a family based club. Not all club
In 2023, the Byron Bay Malibu Club will be holding its 42nd Annual Malibu Classic, the longest running single fin longboard event in Australia. The event will be held on the 29th and 30th of April, 2023. We expect to have over 150 competitors again this year, together with their families and a large number of spectators.
All money received by the Club from our sponsors goes directly to these organisations. In the past 40 years, with the support of generous sponsors, we have donated well in excess of $300,000 to local organisations and charities.
