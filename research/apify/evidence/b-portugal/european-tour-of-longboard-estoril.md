The ESF was formed in 1972,when surfing in Europe was still a new and undeveloped activity….when each Country was ‘going it alone’ in organising their National Surfing Championships…before communicating became so easy…before even videos of surfing were available never mind live webcasts. Then – as now – it was made up of representatives from its Member Nations, and was the amalgamation of all that
The Championships – known as EUROSURF, was first run by Jersey in 1969, with only Men and Women Champions. It has now grown into Open and Junior Eurosurfs – held in successive years and includes age categories from Under 14, together with Bodyboards and Longboards.
