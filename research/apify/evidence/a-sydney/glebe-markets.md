### FOR MARKET VISITORS
*   How do I book a stall?
Yes, we have plenty of tables for hire at $11.00 per table. Simply go to our storage container and tap and pay with a credit or debit card.
After you have booked a stall, we will email you more info on your allocated spot, your arrival time, what gate to enter the market to unload and where to park.
Arrival times vary depending on what spot you are allocated. Casual stallholders arrive between 7 am - 8.30 am but there is no use showing up earlier than your allocated time
*   What do I get with my stall booking?
A stall booking provides you with just a space which is usually 3 metres X 3 metres in size.
You need to bring your own tent, chairs, etc. We do have tables for hire at $11.00 each. If you bring your own tent you must also bring your own gazebo weights to secure it.
There are also some stall spaces in premium locations that are 2.4 metres X 2.4 metres in size.
