It takes a special kind of human to want to work for Burning Man Project, the nonprofit that produces Black Rock City and supports the global Burning Man culture and community. We’re grateful for your curiosity and desire to contribute.
Burning Man Project is an organization that supports personal growth and co-learning. We offer extended benefits, access to mentorship, a smorgasbord of educational resources, and endless opportunities to deepen connections within the vibrant Burning Man community.
A year-round team supports global Burning Man culture and community through programs, content, and administration, including the nonprofit’s signature annual Burning Man event in Black Rock City, for which the planning and production begins the day after the Man burns.
It’s a huge lift to support a temporary city in one of the world’s most inhospitable environments. Depending on the team, role, and skill set, many of our staff work during the event in Black Rock City. Seeing and being a part of building the city from the ground up is a memorable experience.
