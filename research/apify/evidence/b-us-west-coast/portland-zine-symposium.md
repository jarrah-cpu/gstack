**The Portland Zine Symposium (PZS)** is an organization that hosts, among other events, a yearly free conference and zine social exploring facets of independent publishing and DIY culture. This event has been held in Portland, Oregon every summer since 2001 and hosts over 150 zinesters from around the world each year, as well as many free workshops, panels, and discussions.
**The Portland Zine Symposium (PZS)** aims to build community between creators of independent publications in Portland, OR and beyond. ## The **Portland Zine Symposium** is one of the largest annual zine events in the United States. It was founded in 2001 by (among others) Eleanor Whitney, Elly Blue, Alex Wrekk, and Nicole J. Georges.
— Nicole J. Georges, August 2010
**_PZS Scavenger Hunt_** by Derek Neuland, 2010
**A conversation between two old school PZS Organizers, Derek Neuland and Alex Wrekk in August 2010, in the sleepy village of Portland, Oregon:**
**Alex Wrekk:** At the end of 2000, some of my friends were talking about organizing a zine event. I was sort of confused because it seemed weird to put a bunch of awkward zine people in a room together so they could hide behind their notebooks and avoid eye contact. Somehow I was convinced that a zine event was a rad idea, and we collected about 8 or 9 people and starting having meetings.
**_Conversation between PZS Organizers Cathy Camper and (Elly) Blue, August 2010:
