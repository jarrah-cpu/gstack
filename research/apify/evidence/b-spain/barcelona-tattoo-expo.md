## Contact
It was born in 1996 under the name of Barcelona Tattoo Show in Sants, and since its inception integrates other trends such as Graffiti and the most underground shows at that time.
25 years later, under the name Baum Fest, more than 8,000 artists from all over the world have passed through the festival, tattooing, painting, dancing or competing in the different areas that make up the festival. We have been visited by more than 250,000 visitors who have been able to see live the art that is breathed in Baum Fest.

## CHILDREN'S GRAFFITI WORKSHOP
The intention of this children’s Graffiti workshop is to transmit the love for art in a very close and different way.
As one of the objectives of this workshop is also responsibility and ethics as well as respect for our streets and cities, cardboard boxes will be used as a mural or wall and the sprays used will be water-based, without any toxicity for the children.

## Contacto
Nace en 1996 bajo el nombre de Barcelona Tattoo Show en cocheras de Sants, y desde sus inicios integra otras tendencias como el Graffiti y los espectáculos más underground en esos tiempos.
25 años después, bajo el nombre de Baum Fest, por el festival han pasado más de 8.000 artistas de todas las partes del mundo que han tatuado, pintado, bailado o competido en las diferentes zonas que componen el festival. Nos han visitado más de 250.000 visitantes que han podido ver en directo el arte que se respira en Baum Fest.
