# Attendee en
6\. WHO CAN ATTEND THE FESTIVAL?
The clinics will allow you to learn and improve your wall maneuvers and technical skills on the rock, in a guided and safe environment. In addition, the festival also offers an extensive program of open activities where participants can enjoy premieres of documentaries and films, talks with specialists and workshops.
The cost of each clinic will be 30€/day and will consist of a day of high quality climbing led by professional guides and accompanied by specialist athletes in the discipline.
For more information please contact us via: [**info@siuranaclimbingfestival.com**](mailto:info@siuranaclimbingfestival.com)
This area will serve as a “meeting point” for the participants of the festival and will provide detailed information about the clinics and the organization of the various activities. The booths of the organization and the collaborating brands (gear demos) will also be located in this area.
