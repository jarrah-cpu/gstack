Sticky is one of the only shops in the world dedicated entirely to zines. It opened in April 2001, inspired in no small part by the artist bookstore Boekie Woekie in Amsterdam, and its objective ever since has been to ardently defend zine culture.
As a zine distro, we provide a safe space for zine makers to create, buy and sell zines. Sticky has stocked 20,000 titles since opening, both from local creators and artists all over the world. Zine makers are welcome to come down to our shop to use our photocopier, long-armed staplers, guillotines and typewriters. They can also speak to our expert zine-making volunteers about anything zine relate
Sticky is entirely volunteer-run; nobody you see working there gets paid. It takes a 20% commission from the zines it sells to help with bills and that, but otherwise relies on fundraising efforts and donations to survive.
