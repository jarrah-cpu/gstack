### https://artbookfair.melbourne/about/ (About – Melbourne Art Book Fair)
The Melbourne Art Book Fair brings together local and international publishers, artists, and designers, fostering exchange, collaboration, and a shared appreciation for printed matter in all its forms.
Established by the NGV in 2015, the Fair emerged in response to a growing global network of art book fairs in creative hubs such as New York and Los Angeles. Building on Melbourne’s designation as a UNESCO City of Literature in 2008, it continues to highlight the city’s vibrant art publishing landscape and the communities that sustain it.
In May 2027, Melbourne Art Book Fair will enter its thirteenth iteration. The program will feature new voices in art publishing and design, alongside longstanding collaborators.
