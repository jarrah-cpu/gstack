Meredith 2026
December 11, 12 & 13, 2026
*   *   [Ticket Info](http://aunty.mmf.com.au/ticket-info/)
Tickets to Meredith 34 are now sold out. I’m sorry if you missed out.
If you would like to, you can join [Aunty’s Waitlist](https://aunty.mmf.com.au/ticket-info/buying-or-selling-your-ticket/) .
DECEMBER 11, 12 & 13, 2026
Featuring a Supernatural Amphitheatre, free range camping, BYO everywhere, permanent showers and composting toilets, abundant food stalls, [camping gear hire](https://aunty.mmf.com.au/get-prepared/camping-gear-hire/), and much much more.
Meredith [started in 1991](https://history.mmf.com.au/) as a one-time wig out for a coupla hundred fun-loving friends. So much enjoyment was had that it went down again the next year, and the next, and now, thirty-odd years later, Meredith is primed to happen again, down on the Nolan farm.
Thanks to all the [feedback and ideas](https://feedback.mmf.com.au/) from you and your friends, Meredith self‑propagates.
Every ticket includes camping and parking and helps many organisations to [do good things in the district](https://aunty.mmf.com.au/what-goes-on/how-you-help-the-district/).
Independent. Since 1991.
We pay respects to Elders past and present, and extend that respect to all First Nations.
