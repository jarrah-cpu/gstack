## Tickets FAQ
### [How many tickets can I buy?](https://blazingswan.com.au/ticket-faq/#)
You can purchase a maximum of 2 Adult tickets in any one transaction.  You MUST specify the names of the people who are going to go through the Gate with each of those tickets.  You CAN NOT use your own name twice on those tickets.  One of those two tickets will be invalid.
After you purchase your Adult ticket you can contact Blazing Swan if you need additional tickets for Children or Carers.
To contact Blazing Swan, use the ‘Contact Host’ option on the Humanitix Blazing Swan Event page.
### [Can I buy tickets for my Children?](https://blazingswan.com.au/ticket-faq/#)
Yes.  If you are purchasing an Adult you will see an option in Humanitix to purchase tickets for your children as well.
Tickets for Children between 12 and 17 years of age are $100 each.  Tickets for Children under 12 are free, but they MUST have a ticket or they will not be admitted to the Event.
Blazing Swan is an all ages event. Anyone under 18 years old must be under the super
