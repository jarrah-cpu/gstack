The Distinguished Gentleman's Ride is an automotive fundraising event. We endeavour to make the ride as safe as possible for all participants of the ride as well as those around us. We have enlisted the help of [https://riskysoftware.io/](https://riskysoftware.io/) to highlight safety issues and address them.
They have also been set up with an email address [safety@gentlemansride.com](mailto:safety@gentlemansride.com) for any queries or feedback.
There are many components to a DGR ride including volunteer corner markers, mid group markers, first aid riders and support vehicles.
A Ride Host is the person/team that is officially authorized from DGR to organize the event in their respective city.
While passionate and enthusiastic, it is important to remember all ride hosts are volunteers. They are there to represent The Distinguished Gentleman’s Ride and work alongside us to achieve our mission in changing men’s lives.
