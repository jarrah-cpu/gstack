### https://seaotter.au/about/ (About - Sea Otter Australia)
It’s where the world comes to celebrate the wonderful sport of cycling. With a 35-year history, the original Sea Otter draws 80,000 participants at the annual event in Monterey California. It is the World’s Largest Cycling Racing & Riding Festival, Summit and Tradeshow in one.
The inaugural 2025 Sea Otter Australia was a landmark celebration that redefined the event experience as the Southern Hemisphere’s largest and most integrated cycling festival, uniting nearly 9,000 attendees and 170+ brands in a world-class event where the incredible atmosphere and sense of community proved that Sea Otter truly is for everyone.

Rocky Trail Entertainment now runs Sea Otter the Southern Hemisphere! We have secured a 10-year contract and future dates for the next 3 years are already confirmed, with the Festival scheduled for **21-25 October in 2026**, for **20-24 October in 2027** and **19-23 October 2028**.
10+ official events, from hardcore Gravity Enduro and thrilling Superflow® to epic marathons, XC shortcourse, junior champs, and fun kids races. Plus, the adrenaline of E-bike Four Cross racing, Cyclo-Cross beach battles, and jaw-dropping Freestyle jams!
