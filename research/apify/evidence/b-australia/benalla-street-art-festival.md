## Expression of Interest guidelines to host a fringe event as part of the 2027 Benalla Street Art Festival
### 2 – 4 April 2027
The Benalla Street Art Festival promises a captivating experience for art enthusiasts and visitors alike. This annual event transforms the city into a vibrant outdoor gallery, showcasing the talents of local and internationally renowned street artists. The Benalla Street Art Festival is run by Benalla Rural City Council and overseen by the community-based Benalla Festival Advisory Committee.
**Expressions of interest close Friday 29 January 2027.**
● Celebrate Street Art, host live painting demonstrations, feature graffiti artists from diverse backgrounds, and integrate the existing street art as a backdrop for music, performances, or temporary art installations.
● Direct visitor spending towards local cafes, restaurants, and retail, and provide a platform for local artists, creatives, and artisanal vendors to sell their work or offer creative workshops and experiences.
● Draw a greater number of art enthusiasts, visitors, and families to Benalla Rural City and encourage them to explore the broader High-Country region by offering unique, compelling street art experiences and tours.
Those interested in hosting an event can apply for a $750 Quick Response Grant or a Major Event Funding of up to $3,000 to help bring their ideas to life.

### https://benallastreetartfestival.com.au/about-the-festival-2/ (About the Festival – Benalla Street Art Festival)
**Benalla Street Art Festival** began in 2015 with a bold idea: invite world-class street artists to paint monumental murals across the regional Victorian town of Benalla. What started as a community-driven experiment quickly captured international attention, putting Benalla firmly on the global street art map.
Today the festival draws thousands of visitors who come to watch the creative magic unfold live. Over three vibrant days, towering murals appear on walls across town as artists work high on scaffolds and cherry pickers, turning blank spaces into extraordinary works of art.
Beyond the murals, the festival buzzes with guided art walks, exhibitions, cinema. talks, workshops and live music, giving visitors plenty of reasons to wander, explore and soak up the atmosphere.
We acknowledge the traditional custodians of the land on which we live and work, and pay our respect to their Elders past and present.
