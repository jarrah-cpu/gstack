Since its launch in October 2022, Perth Design Week (PDW) has quickly established itself as a leading platform for showcasing design excellence and thought-provoking discussions on issues shaping Western Australia and beyond.
## 2023: A 'coalition of the willing'.
The project started as a collaboration, the idea being that there is a broad array of design based organisations with budgets for their own individual annual events. If these organisations rearranged their diaries to hold events in a particular week each year, we have the start of a _Perth_ Design Week. And that’s exactly what happened in 2023.
In its first year, co-founders Sandy Anghie and David Smith brought Perth's design community together to create a unique program of 65 events held across Boorloo (Perth) and beyond. The enthusiastic response from both design professionals and the community was proof of concept.
