# 2026 Theme:
For Tangi Street Art Festival 2026, we embrace Mulat Sarira, a Balinese phrase that means to reflect upon oneself. It speaks about the act of looking inward with honesty and awareness, understanding our thoughts, behaviors, and place in the world.
In a time of rapid change and constant noise, Mulat Sarira invites us to pause. It encourages artists, communities, and audiences to reconnect with their inner selves, to question what we value, and to consider how our actions ripple through the environment and society around us.

*   Are there any fees to attend the festival?
Admission to the festival is typically free of charge. However, certain workshops, events, or special activities may require registration or tickets. Please check our program schedule for more information.
While we support artistic expression, we ask that mural content is respectful of local community values and regulations. Any content deemed inappropriate or offensive may be subject to removal.
We strive to cover all essential needs for participating artists, including materials, wall permissions, accommodation, meals, and flights. However, this depends on the sponsorship budget for each edition. We actively collaborate with embassies, organizations, and companies to secure support. Specific conditions for each edition will be communicated directly to invited artists.
