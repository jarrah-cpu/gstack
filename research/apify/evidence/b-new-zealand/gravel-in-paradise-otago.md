There are two grades to enter this year, Competition (shorter course) and Championship (longer course), with two category classes in each grade (Ride Strong for the Farming Community and all others) and separate age groups for both males and females. We will not be running an e-bike category this year. Points will be awarded by finishing order, i.e. one point for first, 2 points for second, etc., 
Prizegiving will be held in Omakau race course, free BBQ and podium medals across all grades, categories and age groups, with valuable series points on offer. Ian Fahy from My Ride Dunedin has kindly sponsored a $1,200.00 motorhome getaway experience for one lucky entrant
A current race licence that is affiliated with Cycling New Zealand is a requirement. If you do not hold a licence, you can apply at [https://www.cyclingnewzealand.nz/clubs-and-membership/becoming-a-member-2/](https://www.cyclingnewzealand.nz/clubs-and-membership/becoming-a-member-2/). We can arrange a licence for the series at a cost of $22.50. We will email you separately to arrange this.
Sign-on will be next to the Omakau School, Race Course Road, Omakau, Central Otago from 9:00 am until 10:15 am, riders briefing, all must attend, at 10:30 am. Shotgun start at 11:00 am.
Full category and age group information is available on the [entry form](http://www.webscorer.com/cyclingotago "Entry Form").
