### https://www.wellfest.co.nz/about-us (Wellness Festival | Wellfest | Queenstown)
[BUY TICKETS](https://events.humanitix.com/wellfest-27-queenstowns-wellness-festival)
WellFest began in 2022 as a response to the longing for connection after the isolating effects of the COVID-19 lockdowns. It started as a grassroots end-of-year celebration—a place where community, connection, and fresh beginnings could thrive.
In 2024, Natalija Makarenko joined Rick, bringing fresh energy and vision to the festival. Together, Rick and Natalija have shaped WellFest into a highly anticipated annual event, celebrated for its thoughtfully curated programs, inspiring festival layout, and uplifting atmosphere. With the help of a dedicated team, WellFest has grown to become Queenstown’s only wellness-focused camping festival.
Just ten minutes from Queenstown International Airport, WellFest’s unique location is a major draw. Set amidst diverse natural habitats, the venue features native tree species, abundant birdlife, and tranquil riverside views. It is also an emerging nature sanctuary, supporting both local wildlife and the mental health of the community.
WellFest is more than just a festival—it’s a movement. With plans to become a charitable trust, we aim to continue fostering vibrant, connective experiences in nature. From music and dance to workshops, education, and family-friendly activities, WellFest is dedicated to promoting health, joy, and togetherness for all.
