Founded in 1987 by four visionaries, Mindil Beach Sunset Market brought a taste of Southeast Asia's night markets to Australia. From humble beginnings, it now hosts over 150 stalls featuring local and international cuisine, arts, crafts, and services. Operating from the last Thursday in April to the last Thursday in October, it is managed by the not-for-profit Mindil Beach Sunset Market Associatio

Mindil Beach has significant cultural importance for the Tiwi and Larrakia people, with the name 'Mindil' deriving from the Larrakia word 'Min-deel' (sweet nut grass). Historically a popular site for camping, swimming, and socializing, it has evolved from a rest area for military personnel in WWII to a bustling caravan park in the 1960s, and later, an upscale beach-side accommodation area by 2008.
