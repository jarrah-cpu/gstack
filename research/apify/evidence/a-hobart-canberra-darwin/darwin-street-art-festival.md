### https://www.darwinstreetartfestival.com.au/about (About the Darwin Street Art Festival | DSAF2026)
emails on page: admin@darwinstreetartfestival.com.au
Darwin Street Art Festival is based in the unique tropical city of Darwin, on Larrakia land in the Northern Territory. This place is home to a creatively rich and diverse community of artists whose work has enlivened our streets and enriched our lives since 2017.
The Festival has been a catalyst for public arts and community, bringing local, national and international artists together to share their work in public spaces for all to enjoy. Over this time, the Festival has found a place in the hearts of locals and visitors who value the creative energy it has brought to Darwin.
DSAF is delivered by Proper Creative, run by David Collins who has a strong background in the rich history of graffiti and street art in Darwin and the NT. The festival is funded by the Northern Territory Government, and supported by many other valued sponsors.
​Since its inception in 2017 DSAF has transformed the streets of Darwin into a spectacular outdoor art gallery that attracts thousands of visitors each year.
