Starting in 1947 when 8 artists performed outside of the Edinburgh Festival after not being accepted into the program, the movement has rapidly grown into a network of 300+ independent Festivals world wide. A supportive, diverse, and exciting local arts sector with multiple opportunities for artistic growth, attracting local and national interest.
The Darwin Fringe is a hybrid open-access festival that supports the development of the arts community, providing opportunities for all artists to emerge, experiment, and showcase new and diverse works in Garramilla/Darwin to local and national audiences.
