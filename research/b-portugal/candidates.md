## Gaps

The main gap is coverage. The shared search limit stopped discovery after 13 queries, so this file holds music and surf only. It needs a second pass on skate, downhill, tattoo, custom motorcycle, zines, gravel, climbing, van life, wellness, and on Madeira and the Azores. Search results showed no Instagram handles or partnership contacts for any record. Attendance is published only for Boom (40,000 tickets, press-reported). Waking Life has a listed capacity of about 8,000, recorded as an estimate. MOGA, Sumol Summer Fest, Mucho Flow, Paredes de Coura and Primavera Porto take their dates only from 2026 roundup guides, and none has an official website pinned. The NEOPOP 2027 dates appear only on a third-party listing. Board Market's 2026 edition rests on a single event-listing URL. Log Surf Fest has not been seen since 2019. None of the records has a sponsor roster beyond naming partners, and the SURPLUS brand snowball (Nike, Carhartt WIP, Vans, Red Bull, Deus) was not run for Portugal.
