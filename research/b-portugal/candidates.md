## Gaps

Coverage now spans every strand in the brief, but some strands came back thin. No downhill or freeride longboard skate event turned up anywhere in Portugal, Madeira included; the search results only showed downhill mountain biking. No climbing or bouldering festival and no van life gathering came up either. Skate is covered only by brand or league contests (Red Bull 2022, Porto 2024) plus Iminente's urban sports strand; no community skate jam with a confirmed 2026 or 2027 date was found. Sneaker and streetwear is the weakest strand: no Carhartt WIP, Vans or Nike culture event showed up in Portugal, and Sneakers Love Portugal was last seen in 2016. Madeira produced no surf or independent culture festival.

Data gaps: search results showed no Instagram handles or partnership contacts for any record. Attendance is published only for Boom (40,000). The figures for Oporto Tattoo Expo, Faro, EDP Ericeira Pro and Waking Life are projections or capacities, marked as estimates. Iminente's partner roster and official site, Tremor's organiser and audience size, and the dates of Feira Grafica's latest edition are still unknown. Wander Yogi's 2026 dates conflict between listings. Lisbon Tattoo Rock Fest, Setubal Custom Weekend, Board Market, Log Surf Fest and the Porto skate league have no confirmed recent or upcoming edition, so their status is unknown. Sudoeste is on hiatus with a hoped-for 2027 return. The SURPLUS roster snowball found no direct matches in Portugal.
