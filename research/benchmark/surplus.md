# Benchmark: SURPLUS Sydney

Record: `research/benchmark/candidates.jsonl` (id `surplus-sydney`).

## What it is

- Three-day, multi-venue streetwear and culture festival in Darlinghurst, Sydney. Presented by Supply Store and Nike Sportswear.
- 2nd edition: Fri 16 to Sun 18 Oct 2026. Humanitix lists 17 to 19 Oct; most press says 16 to 18 Oct. [Sydney Times](https://www.sydneytimes.net.au/events-calendar/nike-and-supply-store-present-surplus-2026-a-weekend-of-sydney-subculture/), [Humanitix](https://events.humanitix.com/host/surplus)
- 2026: 120+ local and international names across 21 venues covering music, brands, art, vintage, food. [Standing Room](https://standingroommagazine.com/2026/surplus-is-bringing-sydneys-subcultures-together)
- 2025 (1st edition, 17 to 19 Oct 2025): organiser-reported 15,000+ attendees, 21 venues, 50 activations, 110 partners. [Supply Store recap](https://supplystore.com.au/blogs/dispatches/surplus%C2%AE-2026)
- Most events free. Headline shows ticketed (Rainbow Disco Club open-air at National Art School, Sun 18 Oct 2026). [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net.au/events/surplus-presents-rainbow-disco-club-openair)

## Qualifying traits (the yardstick for vibe scores)

1. A strong, authentic community or subculture at the core.
2. Vibey, welcoming, slightly alternative.
3. Curated rather than mass-market.
4. Brand-friendly without feeling corporate.

## Vibe score rubric (1 to 5 against SURPLUS)

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Peer of SURPLUS. Community-run or community-trusted, curated, multi-format, brands woven in as culture not billboards. |
| 4 | Strong subculture core and curation, but narrower (single scene) or a little more commercial. |
| 3 | Genuine scene but either mass-market leaning, heavily sponsor-branded, or thin on community programming. |
| 2 | Mostly commercial or generic. Subculture is a theme, not the core. |
| 1 | Mass-market, corporate expo or trade show feel. |
