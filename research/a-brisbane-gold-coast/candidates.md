## Gaps

This is a partial pass. The search budget ran out after 16 searches, so the SURPLUS snowball, sponsor lookups, Instagram handles and partnership contacts were not collected for any event. No attendance figures were found. Organisers are missing for most records (BIGSOUND's organiser is commonly said to be QMusic but this was not confirmed). Three records rest on thin sources: Brisbane Street Art Festival (one hotel listing), Woodford (third-party roundup dates) and Flotsam (listing says 1 to 24 May but its press release says fifteen days). Status is unclear for BAD (nothing since 2023), Big Pineapple (no 2026 edition found) and the IMA Zine Fair (2026 date not confirmed). Brisbane club culture and Gold Coast skate events are the biggest blind spots and should be the first top-up searches.
