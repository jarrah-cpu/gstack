## Gaps

Bali surf, wellness, markets, tattoo and street art are now covered, but many Bali records rest on a single listing or guide, so several 2026 editions (Single Fin Classic, Earth Island, ORET, Ubud Food Festival, Canggu Run) are not confirmed as held. Deus Slidetoberfest 2026 dates were still not found; the 2025 edition ran 17 to 19 Oct, so the 2026 edition may have just run or be imminent. No dedicated Bali skate event, sauna or ice-bath community event, or Jakarta zine fair or tattoo convention turned up; wellness in Canggu and Uluwatu lives mostly in venue programs (Udara, The Istana, Atmos, Omni). Climbing has only competition formats (SEA Boulder League), and van life and overlanding have no current event in Indonesia. Fixed-gear culture shows only a 2026 crit category and a dormant 2011 to 2012 festival. Several records need recency checks before Phase 2: Ombak, Tropica, Bali Tattoo Expo, JakCloth, Kickfest, Suryanation and Rainbow Disco Club Bali (no 2026 edition found). Soundrenaline: no 2026 source names a title sponsor or presenter; historical sources tie the series to Sampoerna's A Mild, and current ownership is unconfirmed. Only one Instagram handle (@tangistreetartfest) appeared in results, and no record has published demographics, a partnership contact or an event-level Australian audience share; the only Australian figure is the BPS 23.44 percent share of Bali arrivals. Organiser entities are still missing for Kustomfest, BaliSpirit, UWRF, Pestapora, Synchronize, JSD, ARTJOG, Ubud Food Festival, Art Jakarta and YKABF. Other Indonesian subcultures not searched for lack of budget: Vespa and scooter culture, metal and hardcore (for example Hammersonic), and Bali club collectives beyond Potato Head.
