## Gaps

The session's shared search budget ran out after 22 searches, well short of the 40 to 80 the brief allows. Several starting leads could not be checked at all: Night Noodle Markets, Marrickville and Inner West markets and festivals, Carriageworks programs beyond the events above, and independent club crews and day parties. The SURPLUS snowball (Supply Store, Nike, Carhartt WIP, Mixmag ANZ, Motherlan, P.A.M., Distort, Landslide Books, Matty's Patty's and the rest) was not traced, so the strongest SURPLUS peers (club crews, night markets, publishing events) are probably under-represented. No search result showed an Instagram handle, so every IG field is null. No attendance figures, partnership contacts or full sponsor rosters surfaced. Only three records have a confirmed next edition (Finders Keepers, Laneway, Field Day). Second Life Markets, XChange Markets and Glebe Markets each rest on a single 2026 guide from a vintage retailer. The "Sydney Tattoo and Arts Festival" lead was not found; the Sydney Tattoo Convention is the closest match. Sydney Design Festival seems to have become Powerhouse's Sydney Design Week, but no source confirms the rename. Bowl-A-Rama Bondi (last held 2018) and Newtown Festival are recorded as discontinued for archiving.
