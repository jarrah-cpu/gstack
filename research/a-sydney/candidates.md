## Gaps

The snowball ran through every SURPLUS name, but most of the smaller vendors (Distort, Landslide Books, P.A.M., Motherlan, the food pop-ups) do not show up at other public Sydney events in search results, so the snowball's yield came mainly from the club side: Planet Trip, Steel City Dance Discs and Astral People. Several of the strongest SURPLUS peers are community crews whose status is unclear: Marrickville Block Weekender, Throttle Roll and Hazard Festival have no edition after 2023 or 2025 on record, and Mad Racket and Deus Surf Swap may be dormant. Picnic's 2023 closure year and Mad Racket's 2023 anniversary are inferred from founding dates and weekdays. Sydney Design Week still has no official 2026 program after a second check. The Nike After Dark Tour date conflicts with the weekday (27 Sep 2026 is a Sunday). No Night Noodle Markets Sydney edition after Oct 2022 surfaced. Only one Instagram handle (@soft_centre) appeared in results; no follower counts were recorded. Attendance figures exist for only Parramatta Lanes, Lakemba Nights, Throttle Roll and Dulwich Hill Festival, and only Parramatta Lanes shows a partnership page. Not searched for lack of budget: Cronulla and southern Sydney surf events, run clubs beyond Nike, Sydney Craft Week as a whole, Chinatown and Parramatta night markets beyond the leads above, and sponsor rosters for the club festivals.
