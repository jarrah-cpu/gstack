## Gaps

Every strand in the brief now has at least one Spanish record, but some are thin. No sauna culture event was found anywhere in Spain, and wellness rests on Wellness Rave (commercial, sponsor-led) and one Mallorca yoga festival. No Deus Ex Machina event, and no Spanish event tied to Nike Sportswear, Carhartt WIP or other SURPLUS roster brands, turned up; the snowball came back empty for Spain. Margalef and Albarracín have no festivals in results. Ibiza and Menorca have no non-club subculture events beyond the Cranc lead. Many dates come from aggregators (festivalesdeespana.com, JamBase, Fever) rather than organiser sites: Rodellar 2026, BBK Mendi Film 2026, Motorbeach 2026, MULAFEST (identical 2025 and 2026 dates listed) and Salinas (two conflicting date ranges). Siurana 2026, Wellness Rave's next edition, Crepe City Madrid's next date, GRAVITEO's continuity and ArtsLibris Barcelona 2026 are unconfirmed. Instagram handles are captured for one record only (Siurana), and attendance figures exist for six records (others carry only competitor or artist counts), several of them estimates or organiser claims. Organisers are missing for most new records, and only Wellness Rave publishes a partnerships page.
