## Gaps

Search coverage is the main gap: only 15 searches ran before the shared budget was exhausted, so whole subcultures (sneaker and streetwear, van life, climbing, custom motorcycles, wellness and breathwork, street art) and both island groups are missing, and this region should be re-run. No Instagram handles, follower counts or partnership contacts were captured for any record. Attendance figures are missing everywhere; only competitor counts exist for the surf and downhill events. Several 2027 dates come from third-party ticketing or aggregator listings rather than organiser sites (Sónar, BBK Live, OFFF, Monegros), and sponsors are only known for Single Fin Classic, ArtsLibris and the Vans Zarautz stop. ArtsLibris Barcelona 2026 and the FanziMad edition years could not be confirmed.
