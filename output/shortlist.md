# Shortlist: subculture festivals and events for High Ground

Date: 21 Oct 2026. Source: `data/events.jsonl` and `output/shortlist_data.json`. Method and caveats: `output/method.md`.

## How this shortlist was made

Every active event with a Phase 4 score was ranked on its weighted total: vibe fit 35 per cent, audience fit 35 per cent, scale 10 per cent, accessibility 10 per cent and cost 10 per cent. Vibe fit is judged against SURPLUS Sydney, the benchmark. Audience fit asks how well the audience suits a health brand. Mid-size events score best on scale (5,000 to 20,000 attendees is the top band); open partner access and lower cost also score higher.

Scores come from the records in `data/events.jsonl`, not from new research, and 308 events carry them. Dates, attendance figures and sponsors below are as recorded, with the weak points the verifiers flagged. A lighter model set the scores from the records alone, so treat them as a first sort and not a final ranking. Four of the top 15 (Tremor, Goiânia Crew Attack, Byron Bay Malibu Classic and The Big Sweat) have no published attendance, so their scale score is a neutral placeholder. Places 10 to 15 are tied at 4.10, and MADE Handmade Bike Show (Portland) also scores 4.10 and sits just outside the top 15.

The shortlist covers 61 distinct events: the top 15 plus the top 5 in each of 12 categories (14 of the top 15 also appear in a category table). Of the 61, 28 have a confirmed next edition and 33 an expected one.

## Top 15 overall

### 1. Festival Iminente (Lisbon, Portugal): 4.60, next edition 2027, dates not announced (expected)

*Vibe 5, audience 5, scale 5k-20k, accessibility selective, cost medium.*

- **Fit:** Closest Portuguese peer to SURPLUS: curated by Vhils and the Underdogs gallery, multi-format and community-led, with brands woven in through exclusive releases. A recovery or wellbeing space and its community projects strand give a culture-first way in.
- **Brands:** Fundação Millennium bcp is a major partner (2026), with Galp, Red Bull Portugal, Super Bock and RVCA Portugal also on the 2026 roster. No health or wellness brand is listed.
- **Reach and evidence:** Almost 20,000 entries over four days in 2026, organiser-reported. These are entries, not unique visitors. The edition was skipped in 2025 and the 2027 dates are not announced (the Marvila venue is its home until 2027).
- **Route:** Published contact geral@iminente.org (festivaliminente.com/informacoes). Not an Australian event, so High Ground would need a local partner or to travel to Lisbon.

### 2. Tremor (Ponta Delgada, São Miguel, Azores, Portugal): 4.60, next edition 16 to 20 Mar 2027 (confirmed)

*Vibe 5, audience 5, scale unknown, accessibility open, cost medium.*

- **Fit:** Peer-level curation and community: running since 2014, music tied to the island and its locals, and multi-format with hikes, greenhouse shows and residencies. Run by a non-profit, Associação Cultural Plutão Camaleão. Adventurous adult music travellers, with hikes and outdoor sessions that suit a wellbeing strand.
- **Brands:** 25 partners listed, mostly Azorean businesses and government bodies (FLAD, Grupo Bensaude, NOS, Governo dos Açores). No health or wellness brand. The partners page does not state its edition year, so the 2026 roster is assumed.
- **Reach and evidence:** Attendance is not published, so its scale score is a neutral placeholder rather than a measured one.
- **Route:** Published contact info@tremor-pdl.com (FAQ page) and a partners page. Island logistics are a constraint; High Ground would need a local partner.

### 3. Great Ocean Road Womens Surf Fest (Lorne, Australia): 4.45, next edition 7 to 8 Nov 2026 (confirmed)

*Vibe 4, audience 5, scale 5k-20k, accessibility open, cost medium.*

- **Fit:** Community-built women's surf weekend with lessons, comps, workshops and a foreshore market, with brands sitting inside the culture. Wellness programming includes beach yoga and women's health talks. A single surf scene, so narrower than SURPLUS.
- **Brands:** 16 partners, including Torquay Dental, About Time Bathhouse (2025), YETI Australia (2025) and Surf Coast Events, so health brands are accepted here. Ghanda presented the 2024 inaugural edition (single source; not named in the 2025 or 2026 lists).
- **Reach and evidence (weak):** The 15,000 figure for 2024 rests on a Google snippet of a Surfing Victoria Facebook post. The organiser's own 2024 wrap reports 217 participants and no crowd figure, and the 2025 wrap reports 500+ entries. Treat 15,000 as unverified; the scale score uses it.
- **Route:** Australian event, so High Ground can activate directly. No partnerships email is published; stallholder expressions of interest go through a SurveyMonkey form. The next edition is under three weeks away.

### 4. Zushi Beach Film Festival (Zushi, Japan): 4.45, next edition Apr 2027 (expected)

*Vibe 5, audience 4, scale 5k-20k, accessibility open, cost medium.*

- **Fit:** Community-run, hand-built beach cinema with curated film, music, art and food on the sand, and brands woven in as culture. Very close to SURPLUS in spirit. A lifestyle-led Shonan beach and creative crowd with a family day; the age split is not published.
- **Brands:** 2026 partners include Vans, Ron Herman, Hydro Flask and Corona Cero. A 2026 yoga and lunch session, co-posted with SBY wellness, included Corona Cero and MAMA BUTTER product sampling.
- **Reach and evidence (weak):** "Over 15,000" visitors is a 2019 press figure (10th edition, from Google snippets); there is no recent official count. No Instagram account of its own was found.
- **Route:** info@zushifilm.com and a published sponsor Q&A page (zushifilm.com/qa/). Japan-based, so High Ground would need a local partner.

### 5. Goiânia Crew Attack (Goiânia, Brazil): 4.25, next edition May 2027 (expected)

*Vibe 5, audience 4, scale unknown, accessibility selective, cost low.*

*Vibe 4, audience 5, scale 20k-50k, accessibility open, cost high.*
